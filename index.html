Every recipe in one book

Add a recipe from a website's link, a photo of a cookbook page or text pasted from a message, and Pantry picks out the ingredients and the steps. Or type it in yourself.

Scan a Page reads the text on your iPhone. The photo isn't sent to us.

Keep recipes in collections, like a shelf of cookbooks. A recipe without a photo gets a cover in its cuisine's colours.

Plan the week together

Anyone in the household can put a meal on any day, and everyone sees the same week.

Plan My Week drafts the whole week from your own recipes. Say how often you eat leftovers, and it plans them for the day after the meal that made them.

One shopping list, on every phone

The ingredients for the week's meals go on the list by themselves, grouped by aisle. Three recipes that each use an onion become one line: 3 onions.

Tick something off in the shop and it's ticked off on everyone's phone straight away. Staples you always keep in, like olive oil and salt, stay out of the way at the bottom.

Shared by the whole household

Invite the people you cook with using a code. Everyone sees and edits the same recipes, plan and list, and can see who added what.

Cook in two homes? Join more than one pantry and switch between them.

No ads. No tracking. No analytics.

We don't sell your data or show you ads, and the app has no analytics or tracking tools. Your recipes, plans and lists are seen by the people in your household. A recipe only goes further if you make it Public.

This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from anyone else.
